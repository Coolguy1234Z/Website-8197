Finding Nemo

Created by John Lasseter

Finding Nemo is an animated adventure movie that follows Marlin, a cautious clownfish who travels across the ocean to find his son Nemo after he is captured by a diver. Along the way, Marlin meets Dory, a friendly fish with short-term memory loss, and together they face dangerous and unexpected challenges. With humour, adventure, and heartfelt moments, Finding Nemo is a memorable story about family, friendship, and courage.

First Released: May 30, 2003
Genre: Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Family
Starring: Albert Brooks, Ellen DeGeneres, Alexander Gould, Willem Dafoe
Rating: 8.2/10
Rated: G
