Shrek 2

Created by Andrew Adamson and Vicky Jenson

Shrek 2 is a animated comedy film that follows Shrek and Fiona as they travel to the kingdom of Far Far Away to meet Fiona's parents. However, their plans are disrupted when they discover that Fiona's parents are actually the King and Queen of the kingdom, and they have a secret that could change everything.
