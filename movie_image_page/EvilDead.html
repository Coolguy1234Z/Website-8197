Evil Dead

Created by Sam Raimi

Evil Dead is a horror-comedy film that follows a group of friends who accidentally unleash a malevolent spirit while camping in a remote cabin. As they become trapped and terrorized by the supernatural forces, they must fight for their lives and find a way to destroy the evil that has taken hold of them.
