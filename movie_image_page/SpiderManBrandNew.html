Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Created by Jon Watts

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is a superhero movie that follows Peter Parker as he travels to Europe with his classmates for a school trip. Peter's plans for a relaxing holiday are interrupted when Nick Fury recruits him to help Mysterio face a series of dangerous elemental creatures. As Peter struggles with the responsibility of being Spider-Man, he must learn to trust himself and decide what kind of hero he wants to become.

First Released: July 2, 2019
Genre: Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi, Superhero
Starring: Tom Holland, Samuel L. Jackson, Zendaya, Jake Gyllenhaal
Rating: 7.4/10
Rated: PG-13
