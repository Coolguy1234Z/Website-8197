La La Land

Created by Eric Kripke

La La Land is a romantic musical movie that follows Mia, an aspiring actress, and Sebastian, a passionate jazz musician, as they pursue their dreams in Los Angeles. As their relationship grows, they must balance their love for each other with the challenges and sacrifices required to achieve their ambitions. With memorable music, colourful visuals, and emotional storytelling, La La Land offers a modern take on the classic Hollywood musical.

First Released: December 9, 2016
Genre: Comedy, Drama, Music, Romance
Starring: Ryan Gosling, Emma Stone, John Legend, Rosemarie DeWitt
Rating: 8.0/10
Rated: PG-13
