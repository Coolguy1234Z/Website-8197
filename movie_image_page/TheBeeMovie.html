The Bee Movie

Created by Jerry Seinfeld

The Bee Movie is a computer-animated comedy film that follows Barry B. Benson, a young bee who dreams of exploring the world beyond his hive. When he discovers that flowers are actually plants that produce nectar, Barry embarks on a journey that leads him to challenge the status quo and fight for the rights of bees everywhere.
