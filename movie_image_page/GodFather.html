God Father

Created by Harry Osborn

God Father is a crime drama film that follows the story of the Corleone family, a powerful Italian-American crime syndicate. The movie explores themes of power, loyalty, and the corrupting influence of organized crime. With its compelling narrative and memorable characters, God Father is considered a masterpiece of cinema.
