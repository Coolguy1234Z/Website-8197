PAW Patrol: Dino Rescue

Created by Craig McCracken

PAW Patrol: Dino Rescue is an animated adventure movie that follows the PAW Patrol as they travel to a mysterious island where they encounter dinosaurs. When the dinosaurs are put in danger, the pups use their special vehicles, skills, and teamwork to rescue them and keep the island safe. With fun adventures, teamwork, and plenty of dinosaurs, PAW Patrol: Dino Rescue is an exciting story for young audiences.

First Released: September 3, 2020
Genre: Animation, Adventure, Family
Starring: Owen Mason, Keegan Hedley, Shayle Simons, Lilly Bartlam
Rating: 5.9/10
Rated: TV-Y
