Mr. Peabody & Sherman

Created by Rob Minkoff

Mr. Peabody & Sherman is an animated adventure movie that follows Mr. Peabody, a highly intelligent dog, and his adopted human son Sherman as they travel through history using their time machine. When Sherman accidentally changes the past, the pair must work together to repair history and return home safely. With comedy, adventure, and historical characters, Mr. Peabody & Sherman offers a fun take on time travel.

First Released: February 7, 2014
Genre: Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Family
Starring: Ty Burrell, Max Charles, Stephen Colbert, Allison Janney
Rating: 6.8/10
Rated: PG
