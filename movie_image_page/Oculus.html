Oculus

Created by Peter Cornwell

Oculus is a psychological horror movie that follows siblings Kaylie and Tim Russell as they attempt to prove that a mysterious antique mirror was responsible for the deaths of their parents. Kaylie believes the mirror possesses a supernatural force that manipulates the minds of those who encounter it. As the siblings investigate its dark history, they struggle to distinguish reality from terrifying hallucinations.

First Released: April 11, 2014
Genre: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Starring: Karen Gillan, Brenton Thwaites, Katee Sackhoff, James Lafferty
Rating: 6.5/10
Rated: R
