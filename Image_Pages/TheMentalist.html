Created by Eric Kripke

The Mentalist is a crime drama series that follows Patrick Jane, a former psychic and consultant who uses his exceptional observation and deduction skills to help the California Bureau of Investigation solve difficult crimes. The show follows Jane as he searches for the serial killer responsible for his family's murder while helping solve a variety of mysterious cases. With crime, mystery, and drama, The Mentalist combines clever investigations with an ongoing personal story.

Genre: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
