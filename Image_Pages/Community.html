Community

Created by Dan Harmon

Community is a comedy series that follows a group of students at Greendale Community College as they form an unlikely study group and become close friends. The show follows their friendships, relationships, and strange adventures while frequently parodying movies, television, and popular culture. With clever humour and memorable characters, Community offers a unique and creative take on the sitcom genre.

First Released: September 17, 2009
Genre: Comedy, Sitcom
Starring: Joel McHale, Gillian Jacobs, Danny Pudi, Alison Brie
Rating: 8.5/10
Rated: TV-14
