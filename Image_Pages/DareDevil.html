Created by Eric Kripke

Daredevil is a superhero series that follows Matt Murdock, a blind lawyer who uses his heightened senses to fight crime as the masked vigilante Daredevil. By day, he helps people through his law practice, while at night he protects Hell's Kitchen from criminals and dangerous enemies. With intense action, crime, and drama, Daredevil offers a darker take on the traditional superhero genre.

First Released: April 10, 2015
Genre: Action, Crime, Drama, Superhero
