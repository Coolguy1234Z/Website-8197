The Boys

Created by Eric Kripke

The Boys is a dark superhero series that follows a group of vigilantes who take on corrupt and powerful superheroes. The show explores what happens when superheroes abuse their powers and are treated like celebrities and gods. With action, dark humour, and plenty of shocking moments, The Boys offers a very different take on the traditional superhero genre.

First Released: July 26, 2019
Genre: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Sci-Fi
Starring: Karl Urban, Jack Quaid, Antony Starr, Erin Moriarty
Rating: 8.7/10
Rated: TV-MA
