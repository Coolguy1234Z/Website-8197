Jessica Jones is a superhero crime drama series that follows Jessica Jones, a former superhero who runs a private investigation agency in New York City. While investigating cases, Jessica is forced to confront a dangerous figure from her past who has the ability to control people's minds. With mystery, crime, action, and dark themes, Jessica Jones offers a grounded and mature take on the superhero genre.
