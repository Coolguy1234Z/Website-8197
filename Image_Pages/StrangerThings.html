Stranger Things

Created by the Duffer Brothers

Stranger Things is a science fiction horror series that follows a group of friends in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, as they uncover mysterious experiments and supernatural forces. When their friend disappears, they become involved in a dangerous mystery involving a secret laboratory and a strange alternate dimension known as the Upside Down. With adventure, horror, mystery, and friendship, Stranger Things offers a thrilling take on the science fiction genre.

First Released: July 15, 2016
Genre: Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Mystery, Sci-Fi
Starring: Millie Bobby Brown, Finn Wolfhard, Winona Ryder, David Harbour
Rating: 8.6/10
Rated: TV-14
