House M.D. is a medical drama series that follows Dr. Gregory House, a brilliant but unconventional doctor who leads a team of specialists at a teaching hospital. The show focuses on solving complex medical cases while exploring House's difficult personality, relationships, and struggles with addiction. With mystery, drama, and dark humour, House M.D. offers a unique take on the medical drama genre.
