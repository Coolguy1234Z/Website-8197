Star Trek Into Darkness

Created by J.J. Abrams

Star Trek Into Darkness is a science fiction adventure movie that follows Captain James T. Kirk and the crew of the USS Enterprise as they face a dangerous threat from within their own organization. After a devastating attack, Kirk and his crew are sent on a mission that takes them into conflict with a powerful and mysterious enemy. With action, adventure, and science fiction, Star Trek Into Darkness continues the story of the modern Star Trek film series.

First Released: May 9, 2013
Genre: Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi
Starring: Chris Pine, Zachary Quinto, Benedict Cumberbatch, Zoe Saldana
Rating: 7.7/10
Rated: PG-13
