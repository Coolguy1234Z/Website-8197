The Boys

Created by Eric Kripke

Invincible is an animated superhero series that follows Mark Grayson, a teenager who develops superpowers and begins his journey as a superhero. As he learns to control his abilities, Mark discovers that being a hero is far more complicated and dangerous than he expected. With intense action, dark humour, and mature themes, Invincible offers a unique take on the traditional superhero genre.

First Released: March 25, 2021
Genre: Animation, Action, Adventure, Drama, Sci-Fi
Starring: Steven Yeun, Sandra Oh, J.K. Simmons, Zazie Beetz
Rating: 8.7/10
Rated: TV-MA
