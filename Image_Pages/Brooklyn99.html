Brooklyn Nine-Nine

Created by Dan Goor and Michael Schur

Brooklyn Nine-Nine is a comedy series that follows a group of detectives working at the 99th precinct of the NYPD in Brooklyn. The show focuses on Jake Peralta, a talented but immature detective, and his colleagues as they solve cases while dealing with friendships, relationships, and life at the precinct. With clever humour, memorable characters, and plenty of hilarious moments, Brooklyn Nine-Nine offers a light-hearted take on the police comedy genre.

First Released: September 17, 2013
Genre: Comedy, Crime, Sitcom
Starring: Andy Samberg, Andre Braugher, Stephanie Beatriz, Terry Crews
Rating: 8.4/10
Rated: TV-14
