Rick and Morty

Created by Justin Roiland

Rick and Morty is an animated science fiction comedy series that follows the adventures of Morty Smith and his eccentric grandfather Rick Sanchez. Together, they travel across different dimensions and encounter strange worlds, aliens, and dangerous situations. With dark humour, science fiction, and absurd adventures, Rick and Morty offers a unique and unpredictable take on the animated comedy genre.

First Released: December 2, 2013
Genre: Animation, Adventure, Comedy, Sci-Fi
Starring: Justin Roiland, Chris Parnell, Spencer Grammer, Sarah Chalke
Rating: 9.0/10
Rated: TV-14
