Fallout

Created by Todd Howard

Fallout is a post-apocalyptic science fiction series that follows several survivors as they explore a dangerous world devastated by nuclear war. The story follows Lucy, a young woman from an underground vault, as she ventures into the wasteland and encounters dangerous creatures, raiders, and mysterious factions. With action, dark humour, and mystery, Fallout brings the world of the popular video game series to television.

First Released: April 10, 2024
Genre: Action, Adventure, Drama, Sci-Fi
Starring: Ella Purnell, Aaron Moten, Walton Goggins, Kyle MacLachlan
Rating: 8.3/10
Rated: TV-MA
