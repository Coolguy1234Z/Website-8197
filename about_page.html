About ScreenScore

Welcome to ScreenScore, your go-to place for honest reviews and ratings of movies and TV shows.

Whether you're looking for the latest blockbuster, a hidden gem, or a classic you've never seen before, ScreenScore is here to help. We aim to make reviews easy to understand, while giving every movie and show a fair and thoughtful score.
